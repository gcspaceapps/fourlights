    About this site

    Terrain: NASA LRO Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA) elevation models, GSFC PGDA and PDS. Sun and Earth positions: NASA JPL Horizons, checked against NAIF SPICE. Comms also needs a Deep Space Network 70 m station (Goldstone, Canberra or Madrid) with the Moon at least 10° up: station positions from NASA NAIF. Artemis regions: USGS. Times are UTC; azimuth is from true north at the site (away from the pole).
