# Judging

From the 2026 Submission Guide and Judging & Awards Guide: five criteria. Two projects from each local event go on to global judging. The demo judges see is a **video of 30 seconds or less, or up to 7 slides** (including the title and intro slides); going over rules a project out of global judging.

| Criterion | What judges ask | How we meet it |
|---|---|---|
| **Impact** | Real-world value: could this make a difference to the people it's for? | One named user (the planner after a launch slip) and one decision (which site still works), answered in one screen |
| **Creativity** | Original thinking: is the approach novel? | Real terrain horizons from NASA's 5 m DEMs, out to hundreds of km, and a slip planner across sites and dates, where most entries use a smooth sphere and "Earth is up" |
| **Validity** | Works as claimed: is it scientifically sound? | Unit-tested geometry, Horizons vs SPICE, and our sunlight numbers checked against NASA Goddard's published values for Connecting Ridge (Barker 2021); limits stated plainly |
| **Relevance** | Fits the challenge as written | The Sun-Path Chart is the challenge text almost word for word; the Slip Planner is "compare sites and dates quickly" |
| **Presentation** | Clear and compelling | One visual (the Sun skimming a crater rim, lights going green), one number (usable days), in 30 seconds |
